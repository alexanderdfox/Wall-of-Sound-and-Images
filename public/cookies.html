Cookies & Privacy Choices

Last updated: August 2026

This page explains how Tchoff uses cookies and similar technologies (including localStorage) and lets you change your preferences at any time. It works together with our Privacy Policy.

Your privacy choices

These settings are stored only in your browser. You can change them whenever you like.

1. What we use

Tchoff is designed to be lightweight. We primarily rely on first-party storage rather than third-party advertising networks.

Name / type Purpose Duration Category
localStorage session token Keep you signed in Until you sign out or clear storage Necessary
privacy_prefs_v1 Store your cookie / privacy choices Until you clear storage Necessary
Cloudflare (hosting / security) Delivery, DDoS protection, basic security Session / as set by Cloudflare Necessary
Optional analytics (if enabled) Aggregate traffic measurement Varies by tool Analytics

We do not currently use third-party advertising or marketing pixels by default. If that changes, this page and the consent banner will be updated.

2. How your choices work

  • EU / UK / EEA visitors — non-essential technologies require your prior consent (opt-in).
  • US visitors — you can opt out of sale, sharing, and targeted advertising. We also honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal when your browser sends it.
  • Your choices are stored in localStorage under the key privacy_prefs_v1 and apply on this device/browser only.
  • Clearing site data or using a different browser resets the stored choice.

3. US state privacy (including Texas TDPSA)

If you are a resident of a US state with a comprehensive privacy law (for example California CCPA/CPRA, Texas TDPSA, Colorado, Connecticut, and others), you may have the right to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information and of targeted advertising. Use the controls above or the banner on the site. Turning off Marketing / targeted advertising is treated as an opt-out of sale/sharing and targeted advertising for those laws.

4. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. See our Privacy Policy for details and how to contact us.

5. Changes

We may update this page when our practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the current version. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated notice.

This page is provided for transparency and is not legal advice.
